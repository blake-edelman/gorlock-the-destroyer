# Gorlock The Destroyer
Gorlock is a Mini-Class Sumo Bot

# Specifications
- Dimensions: 10 cm x 10 cm
- Max weight: 500g

# Design
Gorlock will be a lightweight cube chassis on top of 2 rear wheels and a front wedge. He utilizes an ESP32, 4 line-tracking sensors, 2 IR sensors, and 2 motors to bulldoze the competition.
